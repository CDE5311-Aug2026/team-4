# HDB Eco Community — Play Guide

Renew an inhabited HDB neighbourhood through building greening, public green spaces and policies. Balance improvements with upkeep, debt and an announced heatwave. For setup, see the [README](../README.md).

## Your goal

Meet **all three targets at the end of turn 8**:

| Target | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Public green space coverage | At least 70% of residents served |
| Greened HDB blocks | At least 4 of the 6 blocks |
| Community livability | At least 75 out of 100 |

Start with **$12,500**, **2,050 residents** and **3 political points**. Block 103 is already greened and counts towards the target. Housing capacity stays fixed.

## Your first turns

1. **Choose a strategy** in Community goals before ending your first turn. Pick one of three cards; it stays in your hand until used. Selecting it does not activate it—apply the card when useful.
2. **Select a block or plot** on the map or through the directory button. Its details appear beside the map.
3. **Build and green.** Green a block or choose a park or walkway for a plot. Review costs, service area and beneficiary blocks, then confirm. Basic construction requires funds, with no political-point cost.
4. **Read and apply policies.** Targeted cards also need a selected block and confirmation. Policies spend political points and may cost funds. Unused ordinary cards expire at turn end; the strategy card carries over.
5. **Check Funds, then End turn.** Settlement collects income, pays upkeep and repayments, and updates residents and events. If play continues, political points reset to 3 and you draw three ordinary cards.

You can take several affordable actions per turn. Previews and policy review are free; confirmation spends resources.

## What each improvement does

| Improvement | Base construction cost | Upkeep per turn | Main effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Building greening | $1,800 | $45 | Improves livability and protects that block indoors |
| Community park | $3,200 | $120 | Serves blocks within radius 15 and reduces outdoor heat exposure |
| Shaded walkway | $1,900 | $70 | Serves blocks within radius 11 with lower upkeep |

Policies can change these prices. The western car park adds **$800** conversion cost. Each turn also has **$450 base operating upkeep**. Check previews for actual prices.

Coverage counts **unique residents served**, based on the distance between facility and block centres. Overlaps count each resident once and use the best outdoor protection. Building greening protects indoors without adding public coverage. Resident changes can also change the coverage percentage.

## Heatwave, inspection and recovery

- **End of turn 6:** heatwave protection determines livability loss, cash costs and resident departures. Check View block risks before the event and Heatwave report afterwards.
- **End of turn 8:** inspection checks all targets after settlement. Missing a target gives $1,000 support and up to two remedial turns, through turn 10, with the same targets.
- **Finances:** borrowing $3,000 requires three $1,100 repayments starting next turn. Two consecutive turn-end balances below −$2,000 end the project.

Later improvements help inspection results but do not rewrite the historical heatwave report.

## Map controls and language

- **Select:** click or tap a block or plot, or use the directory list.
- **Move:** drag to pan; use the mouse wheel or a two-finger pinch to zoom. Zoom buttons are also available on desktop.
- **Layers:** switch between Neighbourhood, Coverage and Heat risk. Use Reset view to return to the starting camera position.
- **Cancel:** press Esc or use the on-screen Cancel button to leave placement mode.
- **Language:** English is the default. Header and dialog language buttons switch between English and Chinese without resetting progress. The browser remembers your choice.

## Using the AI advisor

Open **AI advisor** in the header. Choose Balanced renewal, Public access, Heat resilience or Budget care. Compare plans and expand **See the evidence** for costs, served residents, upkeep and debt.

This local planning AI compares up to **three current-turn actions** using the actual game rules and the same starting state. It excludes future draws, population changes and settlement; suggestions do not guarantee inspection success. After the heatwave, heat-loss comparisons describe the current layout, with the event report unchanged.

**Review first step on the map** opens a preview or policy confirmation without spending anything. Optional reflection questions explain the mechanisms. First answers are saved without affecting the city's score.

## Saving and restarting

Actions, turn endings and reflection answers save automatically in the **current browser for this site**. Use the same browser and site address to resume. **Save unavailable** means the current session can continue, but refreshing loses progress. Clearing site data removes the save.

**Settings → Start a new plan** replaces the current save after confirmation. Community journal shows previous actions and results.
